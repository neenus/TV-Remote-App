# Privacy Policy: TV Remote App

_Last updated: 2026-11-13 (draft)_

TV Remote App is a free, open-source remote control for smart TVs. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## What the app does with your network
The app uses your phone's Wi-Fi connection to find smart TVs on **your local network** and to send them remote-control commands. All communication is directly between your phone and your TV. Nothing goes through our servers, because there are none.

## Data stored on your phone
To remember your TVs, the app stores the following **only on your device**:
- A TV's name, its local IP address and, if available, its MAC address (used to turn the TV on).
- A pairing key issued by the TV, kept in your phone's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).

You can remove a TV in the app at any time, and uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

## Data we collect
None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It contains no advertising or analytics code, and it does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

## Permissions
- **Local network access (iOS) / network and multicast (Android):** needed to find and talk to TVs on your Wi-Fi.

## Children
The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository with a new date.
